# Threat-control map

Cloud rows updated 9 October 2026 from the variant A evidence of 8 October and the variant B evidence of 9 October (SEC-09, SEC-10, SEC-11, SEC-17, SEC-18, MON-01 to MON-04). Last full update 1 October 2026 (security session; application source unchanged since 27 September, commit `c923958`; evidence at `e6732ff`). Threats come from recognised ecommerce sources (below), each mapped to 42Factors' control, where it is implemented and its evidence. Research and ranking of the cloud controls: [docs/archive/plans/threat-control-map.md](../docs/archive/plans/threat-control-map.md). Control requirements: [docs/SECURITY.md](../docs/SECURITY.md).

## Status words

- **Mitigated:** the control is implemented and its security case has a recorded `PASS` in [test-security.md](test-security.md).
- **Implemented, not yet recorded:** the control and automated tests exist, but no dated security case records them yet. Not a claim of proof.
- **Planned:** part of the AWS design with no recorded cloud case yet. Since 8 and 9 October 2026 variants A and B have recorded cloud cases SEC-09, SEC-10, SEC-11 and SEC-18 (all PASS), and variant B recorded SEC-17 (WAF block, PASS).
- **Partial:** some of the threat is controlled; the gap is stated.
- **Accepted:** a known risk, stated with the reason.
- **Not applicable:** the threat cannot occur in this design; the reason is stated.
