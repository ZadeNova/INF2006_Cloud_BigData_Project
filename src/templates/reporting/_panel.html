Assumptions and limits

Sales are completed simulated transactions in synthetic data; sales value is not accounting revenue. Inventory is a sealed snapshot paired with this reporting cutoff. Generated historical inventory is aligned to its historical cutoff; a live snapshot captured after midnight is an approximation of the midnight cutoff, and this page does not infer provenance the run does not expose. The seven-day rate and fixed lead times simplify reality, and stockouts can hide unmet demand. A product with no recent sales receives no confident coverage estimate.
